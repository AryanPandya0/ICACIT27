CONFERENCE OVERVIEW

ABOUT PROPOSED CONFERENCE

A premier international forum connecting researchers, academicians, and industry pioneers.

The International Conference on Advanced Computing and Intelligent Technologies (ICACIT-2027) is envisioned as a high-quality international forum that brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to share and exchange knowledge on the latest developments in computing and intelligent systems.

The conference will span two full days and will include keynote addresses, technical paper presentations, panel discussions, and networking sessions.

TARGET COMMUNITY

200+ Expected Participants

Fostering collaboration across academia and industry

ICACIT 2027 welcomes diverse delegates from across premier institutions and global technology domains:

CONFERENCE MISSION

OBJECTIVES OF ICACIT 2027

Guiding principles and strategic milestones shaping the conference agenda and research impact.

OBJECTIVE 01

To provide a global platform for researchers and professionals to share recent advances in Artificial Intelligence and intelligent computing technologies.

OBJECTIVE 02

To promote interdisciplinary research and innovation at the interface of computing, engineering, and applied sciences.

OBJECTIVE 03

To encourage and strengthen collaboration between academia, industry, and research organisations nationally and internationally.

OBJECTIVE 04

To foster a strong research culture among students, faculty, and young researchers through exposure to cutting-edge work.

OBJECTIVE 05

To facilitate high-quality research publications in indexed and peer-reviewed proceedings and journals.

OBJECTIVE 06

To align academic and research activities with AICTE guidelines and the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020.

ACADEMIC & INDUSTRIAL IMPACT

RELEVANCE TO CURRICULUM, RESEARCH AND INDUSTRY

Curriculum Alignment & Applied Innovation

ICACIT-2027 is directly aligned with the emerging areas embedded in the revised engineering curriculum under AICTE and NEP-2020, including Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cybersecurity, and Intelligent Systems.

The conference will bridge the gap between theoretical learning and practical industry application by creating a forum where cutting-edge research and real-world innovation meet.

Research Stimulation & Industry Collaboration

From a research perspective, the event will stimulate quality publications, encourage identification of research gaps, and promote cross-disciplinary collaboration among institutions.

For industry, the conference will serve as a recruitment and collaboration platform, enabling technology companies to engage directly with faculty and students working in relevant domains.

ORGANISING INSTITUTE

MADHUBEN AND BHANUBHAI PATEL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (MBIT)

Madhuben and Bhanubhai Patel Institute of Technology (MBIT) is a premier engineering institute affiliated with CVM University, Vallabh Vidyanagar, Gujarat, India. The institution is recognised by AICTE and is committed to excellence in technical education, research, and innovation.

The Departments of Computer Engineering and Information Technology jointly present this proposal to host an international conference in collaboration with NFSU.

AFFILIATING UNIVERSITY

CVM UNIVERSITY

CVM University is a well-established private university with a strong track record of academic events, research activities, and industry collaboration.

The university infrastructure includes state-of-the-art seminar halls, auditoriums, ICT-enabled classrooms, high-speed internet connectivity, and dedicated research labs, making it a suitable and capable host for an international-level conference.

Paper Submission (ICACIT 2027)

Manuscripts must report original, previously unpublished research on advanced computing and intelligent technologies. All submissions undergo double-blind peer review.

Submission Guidelines

  • Tentative Submission Deadline: March 2027
  • Format: Standard double-column research format
  • Review Process: Double-blind peer review

Registration (ICACIT 2027)

Registration is mandatory for at least one author per accepted paper.
